# Sudoku

A clean, minimalist Sudoku web app designed for focus. No reminders, no clutter. Just the puzzle, your daily streak, and your score.

## Features

- **Four difficulty levels:** Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. Higher levels unlock as you play: Medium after 2 Easy games, Hard after 4 Medium games, Expert after 16 Hard games.
- **Pencil notes:** jot down candidates in any cell. The number pad never disables or hides a digit, so you can't use it to guess what's left.
- **Smart hints:** a single Hint button offering *Fill number* (reveals the correct digit) or *Fill possible numbers* (fills all pencil candidates).
- **Instant feedback:** correct entries show in green and wrong ones in red, with a subtle shake on the mistaken cell. After three mistakes, you can earn a life after a short wait. No ads.
- **Streak and daily score:** track your streak and each day's score with completion time.
- **Auto-save and resume:** pick up where you left off.
- **Undo / redo / erase**, highlighting aids, and pause with the board hidden.
- **History and stats** to track your progress over time.
- **Unique-solution puzzle generator:** every puzzle has exactly one solution.
- **Light and dark themes**, offline-first, with optional haptic and sound toggles.
- **Backup:** export and import your data.
